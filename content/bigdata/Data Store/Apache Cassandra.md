## What is Apache Cassandra?

> ![[content/bigdata/Data Store/image/cassandra.png]]                                              Apache Cassandra is an open source NoSQL distributed database trusted by thousands of companies for scalability and high availability without compromising performance. Linear scalability and proven fault-tolerance on commodity hardware or cloud infrastructure make it the perfect platform for mission-critical data.
